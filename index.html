Inspection information

Date and time are recorded automatically from this device.

4 required photos

Take a current photo for each view. On some devices, you may also be able to choose an existing photo. Photos are compressed and stored only in this app's local database.

Safety checklist

Select PASS, FAIL or N/A for each item. Critical items cannot be N/A. FAIL on any item results in REJECTED.

A rejection means do not load or use the trailer until it has been reviewed and cleared by authorized personnel. This checklist does not certify insurance coverage.

Inspection history

Records expire 30 × 24 hours after saving. Expired records are deleted when this app opens or runs. Keep a separate approved archive if company policy requires longer retention.
